Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Last Updated: March 5, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and local storage mechanisms are, how DevForge Tools utilizes them, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

A cookie is a small text file placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Local storage (HTML5 localStorage) is a modern web standard that allows web applications to store key-value data directly in your browser without transmitting it with every HTTP request.

Crucial Privacy Assurance: DevForge Tools never stores your code, JSON payloads, passwords, or developer tokens in cookies or local storage. All tool execution happens in transient memory.

2. Categories of Storage We Use

Category Purpose Required? Storage Mechanism
Strictly Necessary Fundamental site navigation, DDoS mitigation, and remembering your cookie consent choices. Yes HTTP Cookies / Local Storage
Functional & Preferences Remembering whether you prefer dark mode or light mode, and your default code indentation width. No (Optional) localStorage (Written only with user consent)
Marketing & AdSense Delivering non-intrusive advertisements via Google AdSense and third-party ad networks to fund free access. No (Optional) Third-Party Cookies (DoubleClick DART)

3. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on DevForge Tools:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and other sites on the web.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of participating third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Page or AboutAds.info.

4. How to Manage Your Preferences

You can adjust your cookie and storage preferences on DevForge Tools at any time:

Additionally, you can configure your browser to block or delete cookies entirely:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Third-party cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection
  • Apple Safari: Settings → Privacy → Block all cookies
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions
