Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 5, 2026 · Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Core Commitment: Your developer payloads, tokens, and data never leave your browser.

At DevForge Tools (accessible from https://devforge.tools/), the privacy and security of our visitors are among our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information we collect, how it is handled, and our foundational commitment to client-side data processing.

1. The 100% Client-Side Processing Architecture

Unlike conventional online formatting and conversion utilities, DevForge Tools executes all computations locally inside your web browser:

  • Zero Data Upload: When you paste JSON payloads, SQL strings, HTML markup, CSS stylesheets, Base64 tokens, or URLs into any tool on this site, that data is processed solely in your computer's local memory using ECMAScript Web APIs.
  • No Server Logs: We do not operate backend processing APIs for tool operations. Your data is never transmitted over the internet to our servers or stored in any database.
  • Verifiability: You can independently verify this at any time by opening your browser's Developer Tools (F12 or Cmd+Opt+I), navigating to the Network tab, and performing any formatting, minifying, or encoding task. You will observe that zero HTTP POST or GET requests contain your payload text.

2. Information We Collect Automatically (Log Files)

Like most websites hosted on standard cloud infrastructure (such as GitHub Pages or Cloudflare), web servers automatically record non-personal technical connection data in standard server log files. This data includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring/exit pages
  • Number of clicks for aggregate traffic analysis

This information is not linked to any personally identifiable information and is used exclusively for server maintenance, routing optimization, and DDoS defense.

3. Cookies and Local Storage Governance

DevForge Tools utilizes modern cookie consent management in strict accordance with the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for foundational security and routing.
  • Preference Storage (Local Storage): We save your preferred UI theme (dark or light mode) and preferred indentation width in your browser's localStorage only after you have granted explicit consent via our cookie consent banner. If you decline non-essential storage, no preferences are written to disk.
  • No Tool Data Storage: Your tool inputs and outputs are never stored in cookies or persistent storage.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

To keep these developer tools completely free without paywalls or subscriptions, DevForge Tools may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and third-party advertising networks:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info or the Network Advertising Initiative.
  • Our cookie consent manager strictly prevents ad scripts from executing until you have granted affirmative consent.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Economic Area)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users in the European Union are entitled to the following rights:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict or object to processing: You have the right to request that we restrict or cease the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer collected data to another organization.

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • The right to know what personal data is collected, disclosed, or sold.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information collected.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. (DevForge Tools does not sell your personal data).
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising CCPA rights.

7. Children's Information

DevForge Tools does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that our site holds personal information of a child, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
