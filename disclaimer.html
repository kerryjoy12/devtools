3. Cryptography & Security Notice

Base64 Is Not Encryption: As detailed extensively in our technical guides, Base64 is a binary-to-text representation scheme, not an encryption cipher. Encoding data into Base64 provides zero cryptographic security or confidentiality. Never rely on Base64 to secure passwords, secret keys, or confidential customer information.

UUID Uniqueness: While our UUID generator utilizes standard CSPRNG APIs (crypto.randomUUID and crypto.getRandomValues), no random number generator can mathematically guarantee absolute uniqueness without a centralized database unique constraint.
